description: KC Social Walk Club is a Kansas City community built around walks, runs, music, and shared experiences. Founded in Kansas City in 2025.
---

KC Social Walk Club is a Kansas City social community created to bring people together through
walking, events, music, and shared experiences.

What started as a simple way to meet people and explore Kansas City has grown into a community of
people coming together throughout the city.

Whether you're coming alone or bringing a friend, there's always an opportunity to meet someone
new.
