The club runs on people showing up. There are three ways to do more than walk.

## Volunteer

Help make our events happen. Setup, route leading, photos, welcoming people who came alone. Say
what you'd like to help with and we'll find you a spot.

## Collaborate

Interested in partnering with KC Social Walk Club? Bring your group, business, or venue into an
upcoming walk, run, or social event.

## Sponsor

Support upcoming community events. Sponsorship helps cover music, permits, and supplies, and puts
you in front of a few hundred people who show up in person.
