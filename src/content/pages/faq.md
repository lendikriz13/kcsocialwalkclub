---
title: Questions
description: Common questions about KC Social Walk Club walks, runs, and events in Kansas City.
items:
  - question: Are the walks free?
    answer: Yes. Every walk, run, and social event is free. There are no dues and no membership.
  - question: Do I need to RSVP?
    answer: No. Add the event to your calendar so you don't forget, then show up at the meeting spot a few minutes early.
  - question: Can I come alone?
    answer: Yes, and plenty of people do. Coming alone is the most common way people join their first walk.
  - question: Are events 18+?
    answer: Walks and social events are open to all ages. Anyone under 18 should come with a parent or guardian.
  - question: What happens if it rains?
    answer: Light rain, we walk. Storms, ice, or dangerous heat, we cancel and post an update on the event page and social channels before the start time.
  - question: How fast do you walk?
    answer: Conversational pace. Most walks run under two miles and take about an hour. Nobody gets left behind.
  - question: How do I volunteer or sponsor an event?
    answer: Scroll up to the Get Involved section on this page.
---
